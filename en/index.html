FAQ

You might be wondering

The most commonly asked questions about EasyPaste's installation, privacy, and features.

Is EasyPaste free?▾

Yes. EasyPaste is completely free. You can download and use it for free.

What macOS versions does EasyPaste support?▾

Requires macOS 15.0 or later. Built natively with SwiftUI, it runs smoothly on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Will EasyPaste record my passwords or sensitive info?▾

No. You can add password managers and Keychain Access to the ignore list. Content copied inside those apps won't be recorded. With iCloud sync enabled, data only syncs encrypted between your own devices.

How is EasyPaste different from Paste or Maccy?▾

EasyPaste is lighter and completely free, built natively with SwiftUI. It offers custom Pinboard categories, four-direction panel layout, and a global shortcut-driven workflow — perfect for users who want to zero-cost boost their copy-paste efficiency.

Does clipboard history take up a lot of storage?▾

Images are auto-compressed and cached; text and links take minimal space. You can also customize retention duration in Settings, from 1 day to unlimited.

How do I sync clipboard between multiple Macs?▾

Enable iCloud sync in Settings and your clipboard history will sync across all your Mac devices via iCloud.
