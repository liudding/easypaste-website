Maccy vs Paste (2026): Free Minimalist vs Polished Sync

Updated 2026-07-24 · ~6 min read · For the undecided

Maccy and Paste get compared constantly, but they are not really in the same lane: one pushes "free, local, fast" to the limit, the other pushes "beautiful UI, iCloud sync" to the limit. This comparison helps you decide by real need, not hype.

Price: Free vs Subscription

Maccy is completely free and open source, sustained by donations. Paste is a subscription ($29.99/year, or $90 lifetime) — heavy for light users. If "don't pay" is the priority, Maccy wins outright.

Sync: Local vs iCloud

This is the fundamental fork. Maccy has no cloud sync — history lives on one Mac. Paste syncs across Mac, iPhone, and iPad via iCloud. Multi-device Apple users who need sync have to pick Paste (or a free option like EasyPaste that also offers iCloud).

UI & Privacy

Paste's timeline view is widely regarded as the prettiest, laying out images, links, and text as a flowing ribbon, with OCR on top. The trade-off: cloud-by-default and heavier memory. Maccy's UI is plain, but local-only storage reassures the privacy-conscious, and its keyboard-first fuzzy search is buttery.

FAQ

1. Is Maccy really completely free?▾

Yes. Maccy is open source and free on GitHub. There is a $9.99 "donation" build on the App Store, but it is functionally identical — the free version is enough.

2. Is Paste's OCR useful?▾

Useful. Paste can recognize and extract text from screenshots — a clear plus over free tools — but most casual users don't strictly need it.
