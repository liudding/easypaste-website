EasyPaste Tutorial (2026): From Install to Efficient in 5 Minutes

Updated 2026-07-24 · ~6 min read · For EasyPaste users

Most people use only about 10% of EasyPaste (the free macOS clipboard manager) in the first three days. This tutorial walks through every key step — from install to iCloud sync — so you can build a "never lose a copy, search and it appears" workflow in five minutes.

1. Install & Permission

Download the DMG (latest) from the site and drag it into Applications. On first launch, macOS asks for Accessibility permission — that is what lets the global shortcut open the panel across any app. Enable it under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. Without it, the shortcut won't pop the panel.

Also tick "Launch at login" so it lives quietly in the menu bar, always ready.

2. Open the Panel & Search

The default global shortcut is ⌘⇧V. Press it anywhere and the clipboard panel slides in from the screen edge.

  • Arrow keys + Enter: paste that item;
  • ⇧ + Enter: paste as plain text, avoiding formatting mess;
  • Just start typing: no need to click a search box — type-to-filter finds that link you copied six months ago in two keystrokes;
  • Space: preview full text, the original image, or a link detail before pasting.

3. Boards & Pinning

Organize clips into boards (Work / Inspiration / Code). Pin frequent snippets with ⌘D to a board for long-term access — like a personal asset library you can grab anytime. Pin your common replies and links to the "Work" board and your writing rhythm never breaks.

4. Ignore List Protects Privacy

Add sensitive apps like 1Password and Keychain Access to the ignore list, and anything you copy inside them is never recorded. Passwords and one-time codes stay out of your history — privacy stays in your hands.

5. Turn On iCloud Sync

In Settings → iCloud Sync, your clipboard history syncs end-to-end encrypted across your Macs through your Apple account. Copy an idea on your phone on the subway, paste it into a doc at your desk — that smoothness only iCloud sync delivers.

FAQ

1. The shortcut does nothing?▾

Nine times out of ten, Accessibility permission is off. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility, tick EasyPaste, and confirm the global shortcut isn't taken by another app in Settings → Shortcuts. Restart the app once after enabling.

2. Is iCloud sync safe?▾

Yes. Data syncs end-to-end encrypted between your own Apple devices via iCloud — never uploaded to a vendor server. Combined with the ignore list, sensitive apps are never recorded in the first place.
